For a standard purchase, the guide allows 30 to 45 days from accepted contract to settlement. This timeline covers approval, formal documents and settlement. For a refinance, the published range is 4 to 8 weeks. The key difference is the outgoing lender's discharge process. In North Brisbane, a practical borrower question is usually tied to a settlement date, a refinance deadline or a planned renovation split. Understanding deposit requirements

Deposit planning is where many first decisions get made. The published guidance gives a firm base for deposit conversations. Most lenders are described as asking for at least 5% genuine savings plus enough to cover lenders mortgage insurance. The same guide explains that eligible first home buyers may use the federal First Home Guarantee to buy with a 5% deposit and no lenders mortgage insurance. Places are capped each year and income tested. For a first buyer, the practical question is not just whether 5% exists on paper. It is whether the savings are acceptable to the lender, whether any guarantee place is available, and what the file looks like if that option falls away. For an investor, the more useful early question may be whether cash should stay flexible for offset or future costs rather than being committed in a way that weakens the wider structure. The guide also points borrowers toward the Queensland First Home Owner Grant. Rather than assuming how that grant will be counted, ask how the selected lender treats it within the deposit position and what evidence is needed before lodgement.
